How it works

Find. Confirm.
Keep.

Four steps, from a family conversation to a plan someone actually follows.

STEP 01 · FREE

Map the family

Who had diabetes, high blood pressure or heart disease, and at what age. Three generations.

STEP 02 · FREE

Check each adult

The Indian Diabetes Risk Score, a blood pressure reading and a waist measurement show who needs attention first.

STEP 03 · AT LAB COST

Confirm with tests

Only the tests guidelines call for, collected at home, read by a registered doctor.

STEP 04 · CARE PLAN

Weekly care

A doctor sets the plan, a guide checks in every week, and the family sees progress.

The weekly check-in

The guide keeps up.
The doctor decides.

Your guide is a person trained by our doctors. They take readings, book tests and remind. Anything medical goes to your doctor, and the guide brings back the answer.

Priya · daughter
Nanna's BP was 148/94 this morning. Is that bad?
Guide
Thanks, logged. It's above the target his doctor set, so I've sent it for review today. Please keep taking a reading each morning.
Guide
His doctor has reviewed it. No change to medicines yet. A week of morning readings and less salt at dinner, then she'll look again. I'll check in on Friday.
