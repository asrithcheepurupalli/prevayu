How it works

Four steps.
No pressure, no test-first.

You do not start with a machine or a bill. You start with an understanding of your family, then a plan you can actually follow, and a guide who stays with you while you follow it.

01

Map what runs in your family

We begin with your family history, the illnesses that travel down generations, alongside your habits and any existing reports. No test-first. Only the evaluations that genuinely matter for you, each with a clear reason. That map is the difference between generic checkups and care built for you.

02

Explain, don't scare

Your guide walks you through what every result means in plain language: what is reversible, what needs attention now, and what is genuinely nothing to worry about. No wall of numbers, no fear, no jargon that sends you to search engines at midnight.

03

A real roadmap

Not "take care." Specific, practical food and habit changes that fit your life and your kitchen, plus the few screenings worth doing and when. What to change, what to ignore, and what must never be missed for your particular risks.

04

A monthly check-in

You come back to see the score move and stay accountable, because you are well, not because something broke. Your guide messages you before a check is due, so nothing important quietly slips by for years.

The guide

One guide.
Who stays.

Not a different doctor every visit. One personal health guide, an AI backed by real MBBS doctors, that knows your family's history, explains every result plainly, and tracks it over years. It never scares you, and it never sells you what you do not need.
