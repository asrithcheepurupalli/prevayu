Our story

Prevention over cure.
Proven, not preached.

We are building the health company we wished existed for our own families. One that gets ahead of illness instead of waiting for it.

Why we exist

The gap we could not stop thinking about.

Most serious illness in India is chronic and hereditary. It builds quietly for years, and by the time a report turns abnormal, the easy window to act has already closed. Families are not careless. They are confused. There is no trusted first stop to simply understand your health before something goes wrong.

One of us watches this fail from inside the system every week. A practicing doctor, seeing families arrive too late with conditions that were predictable from their family history, and mothers and aging parents dismissed with a shrug. The other builds and ships products fast, and kept asking the same question: why is there no product a doctor can hand a worried family?

So we are building it. Prevayu maps what runs in your family, gives you one guide who stays with you and explains everything plainly, and tracks a living health score that rises as you get ahead of your risks. We start with a single preventive-health center in Vizag to earn trust in person, and scale as software plus a supervised network of young doctors to families everywhere.

We refuse the parts of healthcare that made people stop trusting it. No forced tests. No commission-driven prescriptions. You pay only after your consultation. Being visibly on the family's side is not a slogan for us. It is the only way our model works.
