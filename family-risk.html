How inherited
risk works.

01

It travels down generations

If a grandparent had a condition, the odds move to their children, and then to their children. It is not certainty, it is a loaded probability that most families never see coming until it arrives.

02

The earlier you know, the more you can change

Chronic disease builds silently for a decade or more before a report ever turns abnormal. That decade is exactly when habits, screening, and small corrections can bend the outcome. Prevention works because it acts inside that window.

03

A map, not a verdict

Knowing your risk is not a diagnosis and it is not fear. It is a to-do list. It tells you which few things to watch, which to ignore, and where your effort actually pays off.

Prevention over cure

Act on the risk you were born with, early enough to change how it ends.
