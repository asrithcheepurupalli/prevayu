What the research says
DIABETES

One parent, about 3x. Both, about 6x.

The odds of type 2 diabetes in children of diabetic parents. Framingham Offspring Study, Diabetes 2000.

HEART

A father's early heart attack roughly doubles a son's odds.

"Early" means before 55 in a father, before 65 in a mother. JAMA 2004.

BLOOD PRESSURE

Both parents, about 2.4x.

The risk of high blood pressure when both parents had it. Archives of Internal Medicine 2008.

Families share more than genes. They share kitchens, habits and routines, which is good news: those are the parts you can change.
