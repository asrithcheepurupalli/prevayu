# Prevayu

**A personal health guide for every Indian family.** Preventive health, made continuous.

> Making India health conscious. Proving prevention works better than cure.

## What it is

Indians want to care for their parents and themselves but get stuck at the same wall: where do I even start? Reports come back "high" or "low" with no meaning, hospitals only react once something is already wrong, and mothers and aging parents get told it is just age while silent risk builds for years.

Prevayu is the first stop. One place a family trusts to actually understand their health before anything goes wrong.

- **One guide who stays.** An AI guide backed by supervised MBBS doctors, that knows your family's history, explains every result in plain language, and tracks it over the years.
- **A Health Score Card.** One number the whole family understands. It rises as habits improve, which is the point.
- **Explain, don't scare.** No forced tests, no commission-driven prescriptions, pay only after your consultation. We are on the family's side.

## This repo

The Prevayu landing page. A single, self-contained static page.

- `index.html` — the whole site (inline CSS and vanilla JS, no build step)
- Design: minimal, black and white, big Swiss typography, with an interactive drag-to-scrub health score
- Deploy: static hosting on Vercel (framework preset "Other", no build command)

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. That is all.

## Deploy

Connected to Vercel for continuous deployment. Every push to `main` ships automatically.
