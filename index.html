Step one

Start with your family

Who had what, and at what age. We build your family tree, three generations of it, because your history is the closest thing there is to a preview of your own.

Step two

Weigh what you inherited

Parents count most, then grandparents and aunts. Two affected relatives, or an early onset, moves the odds. Diabetes, heart disease, and many cancers travel down generations this way.

Step three

Add your own numbers

Your existing lab reports and vitals, sugar, blood pressure, lipids, bone density, show whether you are already trending toward what you inherited, or holding it off.

Step four

Ground it in medicine

We map the risk using established clinical tools, not guesswork, and a licensed doctor reviews and signs off on every family. It is a risk map and a plan, never a diagnosis.

The result

Your family risk map

Every condition you are prone to, its level, where it comes from, and how many years of runway you have to get ahead of it. This is where prevention actually begins.

Chapter two · Turn risk into a plan

that changes anything.

Being told you're at risk is not the point, on its own it just creates fear. The whole product is what happens after.

01

A prevention plan built for your risks

Only the screenings that matter for you, on a schedule, plus the food, habit, and lifestyle changes that actually move your specific risks. Supplements only where they genuinely help.
