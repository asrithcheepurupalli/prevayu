What runs in your family

The illness most likely to reach you is already in your family.

Diabetes, heart disease and many cancers travel down generations. If a grandparent had it, the odds move to their children, and then to you. Prevayu reads what runs in your family and gets ahead of it, years before it would ever show up in a test. That is what prevention really means. Act on the risk you were born with, early enough to change how it ends.
