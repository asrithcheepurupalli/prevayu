Good to know
Is joining free?

Yes. The family map and risk check are free. Tests are billed at the lab's price. The 90-day care programme is ₹3,999, only if you choose it, with a full refund within 14 days of the first doctor review.

Do I commit to anything?

No. Joining the pilot means we call you. You decide after that.

What happens to my details?

We use them only to contact you about the pilot. We never sell them. Questions or deletion requests: care@prevayu.com.
